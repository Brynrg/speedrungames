The artists behind the world

Character animation, trees, buildings, terrain details and fire effects use Tiny Swords by Pixel Frog.

We use the original “TS_old version_CC0 Licensed” download, upload 9428013, containing Tiny Swords (Update 010). This is the older CC0 1.0 release. The newer Free Pack and paid Enemy Pack have different terms and are not included.

Visit Pixel Frog and the original asset page

Original files are preserved unchanged. Cinderborn adds region color treatments at runtime, original creature drawings, spell effects, interface emblems, animation state handling and world composition.
